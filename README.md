# From Non-Guarantee to Answerability

## Cross-Domain Stress Tests of a Universal Formal Kernel

This repository contains a cross-domain methodological audit of the Structural Flow Universal Kernel and the exact frozen Kernel examined by the paper.

The study asks a narrow question:

**What happened when the same non-guaranteeing formal interface was reused across substantially different scientific problems?**

The frozen corpus contains eleven public, source-audited scientific application packages spanning failure-regime discrimination, consciousness, quantum gravity, dark energy, the chemistry-to-Life boundary, quantum measurement, evolutionary individuality, the thermodynamic arrow, baryogenesis, turbulence, and cosmological-constant mechanisms. A twelfth morphogenesis / regeneration case is retained separately as an ethically terminated architectural stress case and is not counted in N = 11.

## Main result

Across the frozen corpus:

**the applications changed;  
the shared Kernel did not.**

Local scientific and formal architecture changed substantially. Evidence grammars, comparator structures, experimental burdens, object decompositions, admissible outcomes, and application-specific formal machinery differed across the corpus.

No audited application, at its declared scope, demonstrated a reusable domain-neutral structural requirement that required amendment of the frozen Universal Kernel.

That is a historical result, not a proof of universality.

## What the paper does not claim

The paper does not establish that the Universal Kernel is:

- complete;
- minimal;
- unique;
- optimal;
- permanently immutable;
- sufficient for every scientific problem;
- empirically validated across science;
- or independently reproduced by researchers outside the originating program.

Direct theorem-family coverage is uneven. Some Kernel structures were exercised repeatedly; others were not directly exercised by the frozen application adapters.

## Authority boundary

The Universal Kernel does not determine the scientific facts supplied by an application.

Domain science owns mechanisms, measurements, observations, empirical predicates, comparison classes, and evidentiary judgments.

Application-specific contracts and adapters translate those commitments at a declared object, level, and scope.

The Kernel checks conditional structural consequences of the supplied record.

A machine-clean result therefore does not establish that the scientific premises are true or that their translation was faithful.

## Falsification and revision

The paper identifies two principal routes to genuine Kernel pressure:

1. a faithfully represented required Kernel-owned failure or unresolved burden nevertheless receives structural conformance; or
2. an independently defensible scientific object requires a necessary, reusable, domain-neutral structural capacity that the present Kernel cannot faithfully represent and that cannot honestly remain at a lower authority level.

A current live pressure surface is the distinction between the Kernel's explicitly pairwise executable Coherence machinery and any future scientifically necessary n-ary or global Coherence relation.
